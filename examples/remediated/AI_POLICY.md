# AI usage policy

## Approved tools
Approved tools are Claude Code and GitHub Copilot with the organisation account. Other
assistants need approval from the support-platform team first.

## Human review
Every change written with an AI assistant requires human review by someone other than the
author before merge. Generated files are declared in `.ai-provenance.json` with the reviewer.

## Personal and sensitive data
Never paste personal data, customer tickets, credentials or other sensitive data into an
assistant. Tests and fixtures use synthetic data only.

## Accountability
The engineer who merges a change is accountable for it, whoever or whatever wrote it. The
support-platform team owns this policy and reviews it every six months.
